# Tesla Trophy
This so i conducted a small quiz type event in my locality at vidisha. The Quiz was about science and stuff! 

# Why did i made this? 
ok why did i made this? hmm so me and arjav who organized this quiz we thought why to give a normal trophy that all the ppl give let's do something crazy like something different then we had a idea like why not make a 3d printed trophy so then i just made this!! And i think it looks pretty good!! 

# What was the hardest part?
there were many parts that felt hard like making ridges then making the logo of the quiz thingy also when i was making this trophy when i tried to extrude the text on the trophy body then it was giving me some error i still dont know what i did wrong on that part but yea after that i had to delete a couple of my sketches and extrusions so yea these were the parts that i think were actually hard.
